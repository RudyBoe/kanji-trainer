    Points game

    Right answer = revealed and not marked missed. Per kanji: N5 1 point … N1 5 points (●), half if its reading was shown as a hint. Each word scores once a day; a word from the missed pile scores double. Kanji level up: bronze (right in a word, +2), silver (3 words, +5), gold (every reading, +10).
